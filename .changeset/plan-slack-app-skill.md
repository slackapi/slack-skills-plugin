Add the plan-slack-app skill: turn an idea for a new Slack app or agent into a product spec before anything is scaffolded. It starts from a shell of current Slack features (agent view, App Home, streaming, feedback, and more), asks a few plain questions to make the app the developer's own, then hands off to `create-slack-app`. It works alongside `superpowers:brainstorming` when that skill is installed.
